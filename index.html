One company, end to end

A data management programme for a fictional CySEC-regulated online investment firm, built in eight parts: from the data model to data quality, governance, metadata, master data, privacy and a BI dashboard.

Fictional case. The company, its people, systems and data are invented for this portfolio. Any resemblance to a real firm is coincidental. All data is synthetic.

The eight parts

  • 1
    Case briefIn progress
    The company, its systems, and the data problems the programme sets out to fix.
  • 2
    Data model and databasePlanned
    Conceptual, logical and physical models, the SQLite build and a data dictionary.
  • 3
    Data quality assessmentPlanned
    Profiling, quality dimensions and rules in SQL, a scorecard, root-cause analysis and remediation.
  • 4
    Data governance programmePlanned
    Charter, operating model, owners and stewards, RACI, policies, metrics and a maturity baseline.
  • 5
    Metadata: glossary, catalogue, lineagePlanned
    Business glossary, catalogue entries, lineage and impact analysis.
  • 6
    Master and reference dataPlanned
    Governed code sets, customer matching, survivorship and the golden record.
  • 7
    Security, privacy and retentionPlanned
    Classification, access and masking, retention schedule and a GDPR record of processing.
  • 8
    Warehouse and dashboardPlanned
    Star schema, warehouse load and a Power BI dashboard.
